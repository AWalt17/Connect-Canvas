Why this platform matters

Many art students in the Midwest struggle to find work experience in their field, while small businesses and nonprofits often need affordable creative support. This platform bridges that gap.

For Students

Build portfolios, gain work experience, connect with real clients, and grow confidence in the local creative industry.

For Small Artists

Showcase talent, find paid or experience-building projects, and become more visible in the Midwest art scene.

For Businesses & Nonprofits

Access affordable, fresh creative talent for marketing materials, branding, events, and community projects.

How it works

The process is simple and designed to connect local talent with local need.
